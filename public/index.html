PawNir Legal & Welfare Framework

Community & Legal Policies

A non-profit welfare platform under Kuttawaala & Catwaala Animal Welfare, Bangladesh.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Built for Bangladesh Animal Welfare

1. Our Ethical Commitment

PawNir is operated as a non-profit community initiative under Kuttawaala & Catwaala Animal Welfare, Bangladesh. We collect only minimal, purposeful data required to reunite lost pets, connect verified blood donors, and foster homeless animals. We never sell, monetize, or expose personal user data to third-party advertisers.

2. Location Privacy & Phone Number Shielding

To safeguard pet parents and rescuers against extortion, harassment, or animal theft:

  • Broad Area Only: Lost pet reports and blood donor profiles only display general locality (e.g. Dhanmondi 10A or Mirpur DOHS). Exact home addresses are never published.
  • Screened Contact Sharing: Private phone numbers are never publicly indexed or accessible to web scrapers. Contact details are shared strictly upon consented mutual actions (e.g. verified sighting tip or emergency blood request).

3. 2-Step Math Authentication Security

PawNir utilizes a client-side 2-step mathematical verification backed by cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 stateless tokens. This eliminates the vulnerability of SMS OTP hijacking and ensures that accounts remain accessible without telecom dependency across Bangladesh.

4. Data Retention & Pet De-identification

Once a lost pet is marked as "Reunited / Safe", contact associations on the public feed are archived immediately. Pet parents can update or delete pet profiles and blood donor availability at any time through their account dashboard.

Terms of Service

Effective: October 2026 · Governing Law: People's Republic of Bangladesh

1. Strict Prohibition of Commercial Animal Trade

In accordance with the Bangladesh Animal Welfare Act 2019 (প্রাণিকল্যাণ আইন, ২০১৯) and our founding ethics, PawNir strictly prohibits any buying, selling, commercial breeding, or trading of live animals. Any listing offering animals for sale, asking for purchase money, or promoting commercial puppy/kitten mills will result in immediate permanent account termination and referral to animal welfare authorities.

2. Medical & Veterinary Cross-Matching Disclaimer

PawNir serves as an emergency communication facilitator connecting consented animal blood donors with families in urgent need. PawNir is not a veterinary hospital and does not perform clinical procedures. Prior to any blood transfusion, a registered, licensed veterinary surgeon must perform physical assessment, feline/canine blood cross-matching, and disease screening. Transfusions take place solely under clinical veterinary oversight.

3. Foster Care & Google Maps Directory

Foster care listings and shelter locations on Google Maps are submitted and verified by community members. While PawNir conducts screening and collects real community reviews, pet parents are encouraged to inspect facilities and establish mutual written consent for foster durations.

4. Limitation of Liability

PawNir and its parent non-profit organizations (Kuttawaala & Catwaala Animal Welfare) facilitate mutual animal welfare assistance in good faith and shall not be held liable for third-party veterinary outcomes or independent agreements made outside the platform.

Community Guidelines

Rules of Kindness & Accountability for Every PawNir Member

1. Animal Welfare First

Every alert, foster entry, and blood donation pledge must put the physical safety and psychological well-being of the animal first. Treat all volunteers, rescuers, and fosters with dignity and kindness.

2. Keep the Live Feed Clean & Truthful

  • No False Alerts: Do not submit prank sightings, exaggerated medical emergencies, or fraudulent reports.
  • Mark Reunited Promptly: When your pet returns home safely, mark the report as resolved within 24 hours so rescue volunteers can refocus on active emergencies.

3. Honest, Constructive Community Reviews

Reviews for local foster centers on Google Maps must reflect authentic firsthand experiences. Retaliatory reviews, hate speech, or extortion attempts are strictly prohibited and will be removed following review audits.

4. Zero Tolerance for Exploitation

Any user attempting to solicit adoption fees, extort pet owners for lost pet return, or harvest numbers for unsolicited marketing will be banned immediately with their IP and device logged.
